Adventures can fill your soul

Every route starts with a feeling: salt on your skin, a slow morning, a view that makes you forget your phone.

Two weeks along the shore of Lake Malawi, with sailing, snorkelling and long dinners under the lanterns, and nothing on the schedule but the sunset.

Every journey should leave you a little wilder

We build trips in groups of twelve, led by people who live where you are going. You eat where they eat and walk the routes they walk.

You bring curiosity. We handle the rest, from the first transfer to the last ferry home.
